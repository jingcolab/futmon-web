Read the method and limitations

Ending EPS = starting annual EPS × (1 + growth rate)years. Implied value = ending EPS × ending P/E. Annualized price change = (implied value ÷ comparison price)1/years − 1. Rates exclude dividends, taxes, fees, inflation, and changing share counts beyond their possible effect on EPS. Growth and multiples are user assumptions, not probabilities. Scenario names do not imply ordered outcomes; changing inputs may reverse them. Not suitable for negative earnings.
